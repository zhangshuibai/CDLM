# Third-party code

`puzzle_generator.py` and `advanced_sudoku_generator.py` are derived from an
earlier revision of the Sudoku puzzle generator by Ali Alp (2024),
https://github.com/alicommit-malp/sudoku, whose README states that the project is
licensed under the MIT License. Here they are used only by `generate_data.py`, to
sample solved grids.
